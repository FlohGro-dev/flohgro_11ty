These are my answers to [Knowember](https://robertbirming.com/knowember/) by [Robert Birming](https://robertbirming.com): the same few questions for everyone, so we can get to know the people behind the blogs.

## A Photo

## Name or Nickname

FlohGro. "Floh" is what friends have always called me. It's the usual short form of Florian, which is normally written "Flo" - I can't really tell anymore why I started writing it with an "h" (which makes it the German word for flea - and no, not because I'm short). I think mainly to tell myself apart from the other Florians in my life. "Gro" is the start of my last name, and the combination started as a handle and stuck for my online presence.

## Where in the World I Blog From

A small town in Southern Germany.

## What My Blog Is Mostly About

Mostly productivity tools and automation. It started with a lot of [Drafts](https://getdrafts.com) actions and [Craft](https://www.craft.do) workflows, plus some [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com), and Drafts is still a regular topic. In 2025 I took part in [Halide's weekly photo challenge](/tags/halide-2025/) until it ended, and this year it's mostly been [On This Day](/onthisday/), the app I'm building, plus my [Now](/now/) updates.

I'd like to write more about the non-tech parts of life, or completely weird workflows I built for myself, but 1) I hold back because I don't know if people want to read that here and 2) life is busy and I don't take enough time for this.

If you'd read that kind of post, let me know :).
