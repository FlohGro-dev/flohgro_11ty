The last Now update was in the middle of May, and I predicted that summer might slow the blog down. It did. So this is a catch-up for June through October, and it's less tidy than a monthly update would have been.

## On This Day kept moving

I kept refining, fixing and developing new features even through a packed summer. Five releases since the last update:

- [1.3](https://flohgro.com/blog/release-on-this-day-1-3/) in July brought time zone support, so photos show the zone they were actually taken in, plus an option to burn the date onto a photo before you share it.
- [1.4](https://flohgro.com/blog/releasing-on-this-day-1.4-—-live-photos,-ambient-backgrounds-and-add-to-album/) in August brought Live Photos in fullscreen, backgrounds that follow the colors of the photo you're looking at, and adding images to your albums from inside the app.
- [1.5](https://flohgro.com/blog/releasing-on-this-day-1.5-—-merged-grid-and-50-percent-of-september-proceeds-for-st.-jude/) at the end of August added the Merged Grid - all your years in one continuous grid instead of a stack.
- [1.5.1](https://flohgro.com/blog/releasing-on-this-day-1.5.1-—-supporting-other-indie-apps/) in September was small but worth explaining: the app now shows recommendations for other indie apps via Kickstart Exchange - no tracking, never in between your photos.
- [1.6](https://flohgro.com/blog/releasing-on-this-day-1.6-—-iphone-duo,-landscape-and-feature-requests/) two weeks ago made the app ready for the iPhone Duo, and while I was in there, I finally made the grid rotate into landscape on every iPhone.

I truly appreciate all the great feedback I've received, even though it's not a huge user base (~2500 installs) - it is a joy to see others discover their memories through it.

## Mastodon, and being part of it

Two posts this year came out of thinking about the social web rather than code.

In August, MacStories returned to posting on X and a lot of people on Mastodon were angry about it. I [wrote down my thoughts](https://flohgro.com/blog/disappointment/) because the situation stayed on my mind longer than online things usually do.

More recently, Matt Birchler [asked](https://birchtree.me/blog/why-does-this-chart-just-go-down/) whether we're happy being an ever-shrinking niche social network. I'm not, so I decided to [be more generous with boosts](https://flohgro.com/blog/be-generous-with-boosts/). If I like something, I should pass it on more often instead of quietly favoriting it.

## Quiet work on this site

A fair amount happened here that nobody is supposed to notice.

The biggest change: EchoFeed is shutting down, so I replaced it with my own syndication setup. New posts now get cross-posted by a small script that runs after the site deploys, which means I own the templates, the handle replacements and the failure modes. Yesterday I added Bluesky as a second target, so posts go to both Mastodon and Bluesky now.

I also spent an evening on performance - compression, preloaded fonts, the search index only loading when you actually interact with search, and generated images cached between builds.

## Life

Our roof renovation is 99% done - hard work during the hottest days of the year. I'm genuinely happy that we can check this off and focus on other stuff we need and want to do in our home.

The CAMPs - the youth camps I help organize every summer - were blessed. I'm proud of the team that made them possible, and of the close to 400 kids who came.

After those hectic months it was great to take some time off - we went camping for several weeks and visited South Tyrol (Seiser Alm) and Lake Garda, and spent most of the time on Elba in bella Italia :).

Now that days are getting shorter and big projects are done, I'm thinking about writing more about topics beyond app releases - and will try to tick off a few of them.
