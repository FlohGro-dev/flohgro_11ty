# On This Day Privacy & Terms

[Back to On This Day](/onthisday/)

## Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the On This Day app (hereby referred to as "Application") for iPhone, iPad and Mac, created by Florian Großmann (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). This service is intended for use "AS IS".

### What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not obtain any personal information when you download and use it. The Service Provider sees high-level App Store metrics, such as number of installs and daily active users, none of which is personally identifiable. If you choose to submit a feature request, a comment or a vote from the Feature Requests screen, the Service Provider also receives what you submit. See "Can I send feedback or feature requests?" below.

The Application shows a single app recommendation from Kickstart Exchange, a cross-promotion network for independent developers. Displaying it requires a request to Kickstart Exchange's servers. See "Does the Application show adverts?" below for exactly what that request contains.

### Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Your photos, their metadata, and your settings are never shared with anyone. They do not leave your device.

There are two exceptions, both described in full below: the app recommendation served by Kickstart Exchange, and anything you choose to submit on the Feature Requests screen, which is handled by Kickstart Signal. Neither contains anything about your photo library.

### Does the Application access my photos?

The Application accesses your photo library solely to display photos taken on the current date in past years. All processing happens locally on your device. No photos or photo metadata leave your device at any point.

### Does the Application show adverts?

The Application shows one app recommendation supplied by [Kickstart Exchange](https://exchange.kickstart.tools), a free advert exchange for independent developers. There is no money involved: showing another developer's app earns credit for On This Day to be shown in theirs.

**Kickstart Exchange does not track you and does not build a profile of you.** No App Tracking Transparency prompt appears because no tracking takes place. To fetch a recommendation, the Application sends Kickstart Exchange only:

- the app's own identifier, version and build number, and the version of their SDK
- the platform the app is running on (for example iPhone, iPad or Mac)
- your App Store country, so a recommended app is one you can actually install
- an Apple-signed confirmation that this copy of the app is genuine, which contains no information about you
- a one-time token recording that a recommendation was displayed or tapped
- if you choose to report a recommendation using the "About this ad" button, the reason you select

No device identifier, no advertising identifier, no location, no account, no name or email address, and nothing whatsoever about your photos is sent or used. Recommendations are never chosen based on your photo library, because no part of it ever leaves your device.

Kickstart Exchange's own privacy policy is available at [https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy](https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy).

Recommendations are part of the free version of the Application. If you purchase On This Day Pro they are switched off, and you may turn them back on from Settings if you would like to keep supporting other independent developers.

### Can I send feedback or feature requests?

Settings › Support & Feedback › Feature Requests opens a board powered by [Kickstart Signal](https://signal.kickstart.tools), where you can suggest features, vote and comment. Using it is entirely optional.

Browsing the board sends Kickstart Signal only information about the app itself: its identifier and the version of their SDK. To confirm that this copy of the app is genuine, the Application may also send an Apple-signed confirmation that contains no information about you. Kickstart Signal discards it after the check.

Only when you vote, submit a request or write a comment does the Application send:

- the text you type: the title and description of a request, or your comment
- your vote
- a random installation ID, created the first time you vote or submit something and stored on your device. It is not derived from you or your device, and it is used only so that your vote counts once and you can undo it

No name, email address, account, advertising identifier, location or anything about your photos is sent. Kickstart Signal uses your IP address temporarily to prevent abuse and does not store it. Please do not include personal information in what you write.

Requests and comments stay private until the Service Provider reviews and approves them. Approved requests and comments are shown on the board to other users of the Application. To have something you submitted changed or deleted, email [onthisday@flohgro.com](mailto:onthisday@flohgro.com).

Kickstart Signal's own privacy policy is available at [https://signal.kickstart.tools/privacy](https://signal.kickstart.tools/privacy).

### What data is stored on my device?

All app data, including your settings and preferences, is stored locally on your device. If you have voted or submitted something on the Feature Requests screen, the random installation ID described above is also stored on your device.

### Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. The Application does not collect personal information, and your photos and settings never leave your device, so there is no risk of them being accessed by unauthorised individuals. The advert request and anything you choose to submit on the Feature Requests screen are sent over an encrypted connection, and neither contains your photos or settings.

You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

This privacy policy is effective as of October 13th, 2026.
