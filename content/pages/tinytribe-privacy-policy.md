# TinyTribe Privacy & Terms

[Back to TinyTribe](/tinytribe/)

## Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the TinyTribe app (hereby referred to as "Application") for iOS devices, created by Florian Großmann (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). This service is intended for use "AS IS".

### What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not obtain any personal information when you download and use it. The only data collected by the Service Provider is high-level App Store metrics, such as number of installs and daily active users. None of this is personally identifiable.

The Application has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to any server run by the Service Provider or by anyone else.

### What data is stored on my device?

Everything you enter stays on your device: names, birthdays, due dates, announcement dates, families, parents, godparents and your settings. This information is about people you know, often including children, so it is kept only in the Application's storage on your device and is never uploaded.

Deleting the Application deletes all of this data. If you want to keep it, use "Export backup" in Settings first.

### Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

No. The Service Provider never receives your data, and the Application does not share it with any third party.

The data only leaves your device when you send it somewhere yourself, as described under "Backups" and "Contact Us" below.

### Does the Application access my contacts?

Only if you allow it, and only to help you fill in the people you track. When you link a person to a contact, the Application reads that contact's name, birthday and photo to show them in the Application and to keep the name up to date. When you log a birth, you can choose to create a new contact for the baby. The Application never changes or deletes an existing contact.

Contact data is read on your device and never leaves it.

### Notifications, widgets, Siri and Spotlight

Birthday and due-date reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification service of the Service Provider is involved.

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a summary that the Application saves on your device. The Application also adds the people you track to your device's Spotlight index, so you can find them in search and ask Siri about them. This index stays on your device. If you use Siri, Apple processes your voice request under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Backups

"Export backup" creates a file with all your TinyTribe data. Where that file goes is up to you, for example to iCloud Drive, another app or a message. Once you share it, it is handled by the service you chose. "Restore from a backup" reads a file you pick and does not send it anywhere.

### Purchases and tips

TinyTribe Unlimited and tips are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. The Service Provider receives no payment details and no personal information, only the App Store's anonymous sales reports. The Application asks the App Store on your device whether the purchase is owned.

### Contacting the Service Provider

If you use "Send feedback" in Settings, your mail app opens with a new message that already contains the Application's version and your iOS version. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. Your email is only used to answer you.

### Children

The Application is not directed at children. It is meant for adults who want to keep track of the children and pregnancies of friends and family.

### Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because the Application does not collect personal information and your data never leaves your device unless you send it yourself, there is no risk of it being accessed by unauthorised individuals through the Service Provider. Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as its passcode and data protection.

### Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. The Service Provider will notify you of any changes to their Privacy Policy by updating this page with the new Privacy Policy. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.

This privacy policy is effective as of October 7th, 2026.

### Your Consent

By using the Application, you are consenting to the processing of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy now and as amended by the Service Provider.

### Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, please contact the Service Provider via email at [tinytribe@flohgro.com](mailto:tinytribe@flohgro.com).
